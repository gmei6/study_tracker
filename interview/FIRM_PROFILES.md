## Mock Interview Protocol

Run monthly once drilling starts; weekly from Jun 2027:

1. Pick a target firm profile; assemble 1 round from its emphasis areas (mix of Q-IDs, ≥ 2 categories).
2. Realistic conditions: timed, spoken aloud, no notes, no pauses.
3. Score: correct / partially / failed per question + time vs target.
4. Log the block in `DRILL_RESULTS.md` tagged `MOCK:{firm}`; failures → MISTAKES.md root-cause analysis.
